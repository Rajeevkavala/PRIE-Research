## 7. Final Evidence-Supported Research Gap

> **The literature demonstrates that student career preparation and placement readiness systems suffer from severe multi-dimensional fragmentation, static point-in-time modeling, and a pervasive descriptive-to-prescriptive divide. While isolated sub-problems (tabular classification, semantic resume retrieval, mock conversational turns, and curricular pathing) have achieved narrow algorithmic maturity, existing research provides no unified, continuous intelligence engine that synthesizes heterogeneous multi-modal telemetry (spatial resume layouts, real-time interview prosody and code execution, and dynamic longitudinal learning trajectories) into an actionable, counterfactual-driven closed-loop readiness ecosystem.**

The fragmentation claim was explicitly deconstructed across ten (10) concrete dimensions (Prediction, ATS, Interview, Recommendation, Learning Analytics, Explainability, Modalities, Architecture, Evaluation, and Stakeholders).

## 8. Final Research Problem

- **Problem in Literature**: Existing systems operate as disjointed, open-loop point solutions that evaluate resumes without verifying coding ability, score interview prosody without assessing technical correctness, and predict placement failure without generating actionable, distance-constrained counterfactual action plans [P01, P04, P11, P14, P18, P28, P38].
- **Proposed PRIE Direction**: ScholarCamp / PRIE investigates the mathematical formalization of a continuous 22-dimensional placement readiness state vector $\mathbf{s}_t$, synchronized within a multi-agent Triangular Placement Digital Twin (P41) that closes the loop between diagnostic assessment and distance-constrained counterfactual remediation (P19).

## 11. Hypotheses

Six quantitative, empirically testable hypotheses were established:
- `H1`: LayoutLMv3 achieves statistically significantly higher Entity F1 on multi-column resumes than linear scrapers (Paired $t$-test / Wilcoxon, $p < 0.01$).
- `H2`: PRIE multimodal interview scoring exhibits strong positive correlation with human recruiter panels ($r \ge 0.82$, Student's $t$-test on $\rho$, $p < 0.01$).
- `H3`: Temporal Fusion Transformers achieve statistically significant PR-AUC uplift over static CatBoost classifiers (DeLong's test, $p < 0.01$).
- `H4`: Prescriptive counterfactuals produce significantly higher student task completion than TreeSHAP (Two-proportion $z$-test, $p < 0.01$).
- `H5`: Causal Concept AQG achieves Item Discrimination $D \ge 0.80$, significantly outperforming zero-shot LLM prompts (Two-sample $t$-test, $p < 0.01$).
- `H6`: Students preparing via the PRIE closed-loop digital twin achieve significantly higher campus placement offer conversion rates than control cohorts (Two-sample $z$-test of proportions / Cox regression, $p < 0.01$).

## 17. Evidence-Quality Issues Documented

1. **Small Sample Sizes**: 14 studies evaluated cohorts under 1,000 students (P01 with N=215, P03 with N=65, P27 with N=48).
2. **Class Imbalance Neglect**: Early studies (P01, P07) report unweighted accuracy without minority-class recall or PR-AUC.
3. **Template Boilerplate in Published Literature**: P38 (PrepWise) contains unedited template placeholder text in its conclusion, requiring careful extraction of only verified architectural components.
4. **Scarcity of Public Benchmarks**: 86.4% of studies use private, inaccessible institutional databases, confirming the urgent need for open benchmark frameworks.

---

## 18. Remaining Uncertainties

1. **Long-Term Longitudinal Adherence**: Whether students sustain weekly practice adherence over 16 consecutive weeks or experience mid-semester disengagement requires empirical validation during prospective trials (Phase 08).
2. **Cross-Institutional Generalizability**: The degree of domain adaptation required when transferring pre-trained models from Tier-1 institutions to rural Tier-3 colleges remains to be empirically quantified.
3. **Cloud API Pricing & Latency Drift**: Long-term operational costs and latency stability of commercial foundation model APIs (Gemini 1.5 Flash) under campus-wide concurrent exam loads require continuous monitoring.
