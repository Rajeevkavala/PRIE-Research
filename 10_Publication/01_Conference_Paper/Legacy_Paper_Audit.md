## 1. Audit Taxonomy & Status Definitions

Every section, paragraph, equation, figure, and table in the legacy manuscript has been audited against the authoritative research repository (Phases 01–09) and assigned one of the following eight formal statuses:
- **`VALID`**: Content is directly supported by verified evidence in Phases 01–09 with exact numerical and methodological alignment.
- **`OUTDATED`**: Content was based on an early project conceptualization that was superseded by later phases (e.g., Phase 05 architecture or Phase 06 methodology).
- **`PARTIALLY_VALID`**: The conceptual foundation is sound, but specific numbers, qualifiers, or citations are distorted or unverified.
- **`UNSUPPORTED`**: Claims are made that lack any supporting empirical experiment or literature citation in the repository.
- **`INCONSISTENT`**: Numbers, feature names, or architectural topologies disagree with other sections or with Phase 07/08/09 artifacts.
- **`FABRICATED_OR_UNVERIFIED`**: Numbers, claims, or citations that cannot be traced to any empirical log in the repository.
- **`MISSING`**: Required scientific content mandated by IEEE standards or Phase 03/06/09 findings (e.g., explicit research questions, pre-registered hypotheses, formal threat analysis) that is absent from the legacy manuscript.
- **`NEEDS_REWRITE`**: The section requires a complete from-scratch scientific reconstruction rather than line editing.
