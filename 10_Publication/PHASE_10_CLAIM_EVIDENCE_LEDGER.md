## 1. Executive Summary

This ledger audits every scientifically meaningful claim in `paper.tex` across all sections of the final manuscript. Each entry indexes the exact manuscript location, statement, taxonomic claim type, source evidence phase, concrete source file and section, evidence status, confidence level, and corrective or preservative action taken.

**Audited Status Categories**:
* `SUPPORTED`: Fully substantiated by Phase 01–09 evidence ledgers.
* `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`: Substantiated by heuristic/baseline evidence; deep learning weights or live human cohort pending.
* `OVERSTATED`: Rhetoric exceeded empirical data (corrected during audit).
* `AMBIGUOUS`: Lacked explicit context or population definition (clarified during audit).
* `UNSUPPORTED`: Zero evidence in research repository (purged/corrected).
