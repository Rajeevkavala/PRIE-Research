## 4. Synthesis of Emerging Candidate Research Opportunities

Phase 02 cross-paper synthesis has successfully identified four (4) foundational candidate research opportunities to be formally evaluated and structured during Phase 03:

1. **Closed-Loop Placement Digital Twin Architecture**:
   - *Observed Literature Void*: 95% of published systems isolate resume screening, interview simulation, learning analytics, and course recommendation.
   - *Candidate Opportunity*: Formalizing a synchronized, multi-agent digital twin (extending P41) where resume deficits directly generate customized interview questions and remedial curricular pathways.
2. **Prescriptive Counterfactual Career Remediation**:
   - *Observed Literature Void*: Existing predictive models (TreeSHAP in P18, P22) provide descriptive diagnostics without actionable recourse.
   - *Candidate Opportunity*: Integrating constrained DiCE counterfactual optimization (P19) to quantify the exact, minimal intervenable actions (study hours, LeetCode milestones) that flip a student's placement status.
3. **Multi-Horizon Dynamic Learning Analytics with RL Intervention Policies**:
   - *Observed Literature Void*: Academic warning dashboards rely on manual, delayed advisor intervention.
   - *Candidate Opportunity*: Coupling Temporal Fusion Transformers (P44) for multi-horizon semester risk forecasting with safety-constrained Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) reinforcement learning agents to automate adaptive pedagogical nudges.
4. **Layout-Invariant Multimodal ATS with Causal Concept AQG**:
   - *Observed Literature Void*: Multi-column resumes suffer catastrophic text interleaving under standard parsers, and automated quiz generators produce pedagogically shallow or unverified questions.
   - *Candidate Opportunity*: Merging LayoutLMv3 spatial 2D document processing (P42) with dual-encoder semantic ranking (P17) and causal concept dependency graphs (P25) to generate scaffolded, hallucination-free assessment items.
