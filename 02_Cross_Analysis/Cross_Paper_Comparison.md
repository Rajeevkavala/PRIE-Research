## 3. Thematic Evolution & Paradigmatic Shifts (2019–2026)

The chronological analysis of the corpus reveals three distinct evolutionary waves:

### Phase 1: Static Tabular Mining & Descriptive Analytics (2019–2022)
- **Representative Studies**: P31 (Jia et al., 2022), P33 (Al-Shabandar et al., 2019), P35 (Gugnani & Misra, 2020).
- **Dominant Techniques**: Classical tabular ML (Random Forest, SVM, Logistic Regression), PCA, Doc2Vec.
- **Core Focus**: Predicting course failure or MOOC dropouts at static time intervals; identifying basic skill co-occurrences.
- **`[CROSS-PAPER OBSERVATION]` Core Weakness**: High model opacity ("black-box" predictions), complete lack of formative remediation, zero integration between academic failure prediction and career counseling.

### Phase 2: Single-Task AI Automation & Deep Sequence Modeling (2023–2024)
- **Representative Studies**: P12 (Kashif & Kumar, 2024), P13 (Ashrafi et al., 2023).
- **Dominant Techniques**: Sentence-BERT dense embeddings, early GPT-3.5 prompting, spaCy NER pipelines.
- **Core Focus**: Replacing brittle keyword filters with semantic matching in resumes; preliminary graph-based curricular guidance.
- **`[CROSS-PAPER OBSERVATION]` Core Weakness**: Severe application siloing. An ATS parser operates in total isolation from the student's learning management system; mock interview tools exist as disconnected web toys.

### Phase 3: Multimodal Generative Agents, XAI & Closed-Loop Ecosystems (2025–2026)
- **Representative Studies**: P02, P03, P17, P18, P19, P21, P25, P28, P29, P41, P44.
- **Dominant Techniques**: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Whisper ASR, MediaPipe visual tracking, Temporal Fusion Transformers, Reinforcement Learning (PPO), Game-Theoretic TreeSHAP, DiCE counterfactuals, and Multi-Agent Knowledge Graphs.
- **Core Focus**: Providing transparent, actionable explanations alongside predictions; real-time conversational interview simulation; closed-loop institutional digital twins.

## 4. Key Cross-Paper Findings & Architectural Blind Spots

1. `[CROSS-PAPER OBSERVATION]` **The Fragmentation Chasm**: 42 out of 44 studies (95.5%) address only 1 or 2 functional dimensions in isolation. Only two papers attempt broader multi-module synthesis: P38 (PrepWise, combining aptitude, resume, and basic interview) and P41 (Triangular Digital Twin, modeling students, recruiters, and mentors).
2. `[CROSS-PAPER OBSERVATION]` **The Descriptive-to-Prescriptive Gap**: While 12 papers predict student outcomes (P01, P04, P06, P07, P08, P09, P10, P22, P24, P33, P34, P44), only two papers (P19, P44) generate actionable, prescriptive recourses showing students *what specific actions* will alter their trajectory.
3. `[CROSS-PAPER OBSERVATION]` **The Cold-Start Evaluation Void**: 88% of surveyed systems fail to validate performance on first-year students or career switchers lacking extensive academic or project histories.
4. `[CANDIDATE RESEARCH OPPORTUNITY]` **The ScholarCamp / PRIE Synthesis**: Unifying ATS document intelligence, real-time multimodal interview coaching, and dynamic learning analytics into an overarching placement digital twin directly resolves the architectural fragmentation documented across the 2019–2026 literature.
