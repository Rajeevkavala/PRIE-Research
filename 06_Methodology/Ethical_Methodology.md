## 1. Ethical Imperatives in Educational Machine Learning

Predictive models in higher education carry high psychological and professional stakes. An algorithm that categorizes an undergraduate student as "unplaceable" risks inducing despair, academic disengagement, or unfair exclusion from corporate campus drives (`Paper02`, `Paper06`).

PRIE enforces an ethical charter grounded in four foundational pillars:
1. **Prescriptive Recourse over Fatalistic Labeling**: PRIE strictly prohibits presenting a negative prediction without an accompanying, feasible counterfactual recourse roadmap (`DD-003`).
2. **Human-in-the-Loop Authority**: The AI engine is strictly decision-supportive; under no circumstances can PRIE automatically bar a student from corporate recruitment drives without human faculty review.
3. **Transparent & Contestable Inferences**: Every prediction exposes its TreeSHAP drivers, enabling students to contest data inaccuracies (e.g., unrecorded external projects).
4. **Data Minimization & Dignity**: Only skills and behaviors strictly relevant to professional engineering competencies are measured; personal lifestyle habits are strictly excluded.
