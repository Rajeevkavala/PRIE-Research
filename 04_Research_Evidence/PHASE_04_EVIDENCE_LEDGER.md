## 1. Epistemological Taxonomy & Tagging Standards

Every evidentiary entry in this Master Ledger is classified according to the mandatory nine-tier evidence taxonomy:

- **`E1` — Author-Stated Evidence**: Direct empirical metric, benchmark, or finding explicitly stated in the primary peer-reviewed paper.
- **`E2` — Author-Stated Limitation**: Explicit boundary, data constraint, or vulnerability acknowledged by the original authors.
- **`E3` — Author-Stated Future Work**: Specific research trajectory or open challenge articulated in the primary paper's conclusion.
- **`E4` — Cross-Paper Evidence**: Systematic pattern, consensus, divergence, or conflict observed across multiple independent studies.
- **`E5` — Phase 03 Validated Gap Evidence**: Explicit gap validated in `03_Research_Problem/Gap_Validation.md` (`RG1`–`RG8`).
- **`E6` — Agent Interpretation**: Analytical synthesis, methodological critique, or structural deduction.
- **`E7` — PRIE Proposed Design Decision**: Hypothesis-driven design decision requiring future empirical validation.
- **`E8` — Existing Implementation Fact**: Verified software architecture or telemetry pipeline currently present in code.
- **`E9` — Experimentally Validated Evidence**: Empirically proven finding from completed, controlled experimental protocols.
