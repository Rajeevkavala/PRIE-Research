> **Purpose**: Rigorous multi-source cross-validation comparing legacy `References.bib`, `Downloaded_Papers_Catalog.md`, individual `.bib` files, and **Ground-Truth PDF Text** across all 44 downloaded papers plus the 4 missing candidate papers.

## 1. Executive Summary & Critical Audit Discovery

A rigorous line-by-line inspection of the actual downloaded PDFs against the legacy BibTeX files reveals that **the previous AI agent generated extensive synthetic/placeholder author names and approximate titles** in `References.bib` and individual `.bib` files:

1. **Synthetic Author Names**:
   - Multiple papers had real student, faculty, and institutional author names completely erased and replaced with fictitious names (e.g. `Paper01`: actual authors **Shikha Pachouly & D.S. Bormane** replaced with "Olipas"; `Paper03`: actual authors **Prof. Jayshree Pawar, Swaranjith Gudelli, Shravan Chavan, Sarvesh Bhoite** replaced with "Sharma & Gupta"; `Paper04`: actual authors **Ganesh Vasant Padole & Prof. Abhay Yeole** replaced with "Patel & Nair"; `Paper05`: actual author team **Lim Seong Pek et al.** replaced with "Chen & Hwang"; `Paper06`: actual author team **Nesrine Mezhoudi et al.** replaced with "Senthil & Kumar"; `Paper13`: actual authors **Saeed Ashrafi et al.** replaced with "Zhang & Wang"; `Paper16`: actual author **Yaqian Zheng et al.** replaced with "Tan & Wu").
2. **Paper Content Mismatch**:
   - `Paper11_consortium2025resume.pdf`: BibTeX claimed this was "Resume Parser and Auto-Formatter Using NLP" by "Academic Engineering Consortium". The actual PDF is **"Web Scraping for Job Listings Using Python and BeautifulSoup" by Dr. Reeta Mishra** (IILM University).
3. **Venue & Publication Timing**:
   - Several papers were published in different venues or conference proceedings than indicated in the legacy bibs (e.g. `Paper25` actually appeared in IEEE MIPR 2025; `Paper09` in IEEE ICETAS 2019; `Paper31` in Complex & Intelligent Systems 2022).

**Resolution Directive**: The **Primary PDF Text** is the ground truth. Every research note built during this Phase 01 Rebuild will cite the authentic authors, verified titles, true institutional affiliations, and actual published metrics from the PDF.

## 4. Rebuild Protocol Requirements

1. **Author Integrity**: In every newly written research note, the authors MUST be the real individuals listed on PDF page 1, with their actual university / laboratory affiliations.
2. **Title Integrity**: The title MUST match the printed title in the PDF header.
3. **No Synthetic Extrapolations**: If a paper is a 4-page conference extended abstract or student project report, it must be reported accurately as such, without exaggerating its dataset or methodology.
