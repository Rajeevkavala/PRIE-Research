# Digital Twin

The source plan presents the employability digital twin as a future extension rather than a fully profiled primary paper. Proposed model: a student skill-state representation linked to a dynamic predictive industry-skill graph, used for scenario forecasting and long-term prescriptive roadmaps. See [future ideas](../future-ideas.md) and [novelty positioning](../novelty.md). Do not state performance claims until a primary digital-twin source and empirical evaluation are added.
