# Verified Target Publication Venues

The following venues have been verified for thematic alignment with Educational Data Mining, Learning Analytics, and Artificial Intelligence in Education:

---

## 1. Primary Target Venues

### Target 1: IEEE Transactions on Learning Technologies (TLT)
- **Publisher:** IEEE Computer Society
- **Indexing:** SCI-Expanded, Scopus (Q1, Impact Factor: ~4.2)
- **Submission Type:** Journal Track / Special Issue
- **Page Limit:** 10–12 pages in IEEE double-column format
- **Review Process:** Double-blind peer review (2–3 rounds)
- **Fees:** Open Access optional; no mandatory publication fee for standard subscription track
- **Status:** **Primary Target (Journal)**

### Target 2: International Conference on Educational Data Mining (EDM 2025/2026)
- **Publisher:** International Educational Data Mining Society
- **Indexing:** Scopus, DBLP
- **Page Limit:** Full Paper (8–10 pages + references)
- **Template:** Standard EDM LaTeX Template
- **Review Process:** Double-blind, 3 expert reviewers
- **Fees:** Standard conference registration fee
- **Status:** **Primary Target (Conference)**

### Target 3: IEEE Global Engineering Education Conference (EDUCON)
- **Publisher:** IEEE
- **Indexing:** IEEE Xplore, Scopus, Web of Science
- **Page Limit:** 6–8 pages in standard IEEE conference format
- **Review Process:** Double-blind peer review
- **Fees:** Standard IEEE member / author registration
- **Status:** **Alternative Target (Conference)**

### Target 4: Education and Information Technologies (EAIT)
- **Publisher:** Springer
- **Indexing:** SSCI, Scopus (Q1, Impact Factor: ~5.5)
- **Submission Type:** Regular Research Article
- **Page Limit:** 8,000–10,000 words
- **Review Process:** Single-blind peer review
- **Fees:** Free under subscription model
- **Status:** **Secondary Target (Journal)**
