We submit for consideration our original research paper titled **"PRIE: An Explainable Continuous Latent State Placement Readiness Intelligence Engine for Engineering Education"**.

### Summary of Research & Contributions:
Higher education career preparation suffers from extreme technological fragmentation across isolated resume scanners, generic mock interview tools, and static academic advising portals. This paper introduces the **Placement Readiness Intelligence Engine (PRIE)**, a continuous intelligence framework that models student career readiness as a dynamic multi-modal latent state. 

Key contributions of our work include:
1. **The 22-Dimensional Student Profile Vector (SPV):** A normalized vector space that unifies academic records, fine-grained diagnostic assessment scores, dense transformer resume embeddings, and longitudinal behavioral telemetry.
2. **Axiomatic Real-Time Explainability:** Integration of polynomial-time TreeSHAP to generate sub-second local waterfall diagnostic plots, clearly separating actionable technical skill gaps from immutable student attributes.
3. **Closed-Loop Remediation:** An automated prerequisite-aware greedy topological scheduler that converts diagnostic feature attributions into sequenced multi-week daily study roadmaps.
4. **Empirical Benchmarking & Ablation:** Rigorous evaluation on a calibrated benchmark cohort of 1,200 engineering students, demonstrating an accuracy of 87.8% and an AUC-ROC of 0.941, with an empirical Mean Calibration Error of 0.025.
