## 1. Phase Objective & Architectural Scope

The objective of Phase 05 is to synthesize the verified empirical literature (`Paper01`–`Paper44`), cross-paper comparative taxonomies, formally validated research gaps (`RG1`–`RG8`), research objectives (`RO1`–`RO6`), research questions (`RQ1`–`RQ6`), testable hypotheses (`H1`–`H6`), architectural design decisions (`DD-001`–`DD-012`), and pre-experimental decisions (`EXP-1`–`EXP-6`) into a scientifically defensible, publication-grade **PRIE Architecture**.

The architecture codifies:
1. **Structural & Layered Boundaries**: Complete specification of the 6-layer architecture (Interaction, API Gateway, PRIE Intelligence, AI/ML Models, Data/Knowledge, and Infrastructure).
2. **Unified Representation**: Mathematical and operational formulation of the 22-dimensional Student Profile Vector (SPV).
3. **Subsystem Modularity**: Formal blueprints for all twelve PRIE functional subsystems (`M01` through `M12`).
4. **Perception, Reasoning & Action Pipelines**: High-precision architectures for Spatial ATS Document Intelligence, Sub-1.5s Streaming Mock Interviews, Dual-Track Placement Prediction, Two-Tiered Explainability (TreeSHAP + DiCE), Causal DAG AQG, Topological Career Recommendation, and Triangular Closed-Loop Synchronization.
5. **System Governance**: Multi-agent delegation, zero-trust differential privacy ($\epsilon \le 1.0$), fault tolerance, and model serving.
6. **Epistemological Traceability**: Unbroken deductive continuity from literature evidence through architecture decisions to planned experimental validation in Phase 08.
